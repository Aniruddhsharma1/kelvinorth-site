The contract

What counts as a qualified meeting.

Billable only when all six are true. If any one fails, you do not pay and we replace it. This is the same text that appears in our service agreement, published so you can read it before you sign.

01

The company matches the ICP

Industry, employee count and geography all inside the definition agreed at kickoff, attached to your contract.

02

The attendee holds an agreed title

"VP of Operations or above at a logistics company" means exactly that. Not a coordinator. Not an analyst.

03

They expressed real interest

They said they want to hear about what you do. Agreeing to a meeting to make a caller go away is not interest.

04

It was accepted on your calendar

A real calendar invite, accepted, with a time on it.

05

They attended for 10 minutes

The held standard. Under 10 minutes usually means they forgot or were ambushed, and neither is your problem.

06

They are not already yours

Not an existing customer, not an open opportunity, not on the suppression list you gave us.

No-shows and disputes

If the prospect does not join within 10 minutes, the meeting is not billable and we make two rescheduling attempts free. You have 48 hours after any meeting to dispute it in writing, citing which condition it failed. If we agree, we replace it. We do not refund, we replace, because the thing you needed was a meeting.

Cold email deliverability fell from around 45% to 18% between 2022 and 2026 after Google, Yahoo and Microsoft tightened sender rules, and phone connect rates dropped to roughly 5 to 10% per dial after carrier spam labelling.
